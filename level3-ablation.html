The ±Tool Agent-Level Ablation

Claude claude-sonnet-5 · Codex gpt-5.6-sol · judge claude-sonnet-5

The same agent — +tool (with diagnose_project) vs. bare (tool removed) — across two headless clients, Claude (claude -p, model claude-sonnet-5) and Codex (codex exec, model gpt-5.6-sol), under filesystem isolation, blind-judged by a second model (claude-sonnet-5). A bare agent already identifies most faults; the tool's gains are in localization, not crying wolf, and cost. Faults are scored over 3 trials, clean/expectation over 1.
